# IT-Ticket-System

Problem 
    - we are a divided IT team, yes this can be the result of poor management since every individual has individual goals and some individuals want to out shine the others and stand out from others.

This results in 
	⁃	this.problem = results in individuals taking jobs in secret, and sometimes not being able to handle them which increases the failure rate of our tickets solvency 
	⁃	this.problem = is causing a divide which is making us unorganised, which is bad for our departmental image 
	⁃	this.problem =  slows our working rate because when working together as a team we could solve problems faster 
	⁃	this.problem = results in fights / altercations amongst us as some complain that others don’t work as much as they to , because the appear to be doing nothing
	⁃	this.problem = we end up prioritising the wrong tasks because don’t get a chance to evaluate them since individuals take tasks quietly , when time sensitive tasks are given already everyone is preoccupied with their own tasks and they don’t get attended to.

How to solve the problem 
	⁃	Creating a IT ticket system which our department will use to distribute out tasks evenly and fair 
	⁃	The system will categorise tasks according to importance to prioritise important and time sensitive tasks
	⁃	The systems will check who is free when allocating tasks to technicians 
	⁃	The system will ensure there’s a way the organisation can log tickets / alert us on problems
	⁃	The system will keep track of every ticket logged 
	⁃	The system will show which task is next to be solved
	⁃	The system will show which task hasn’t been solved
	⁃	This will keep track of who solved/worked on a certain task

Flow (MVP)
	⁃	organisation logs ticket through WhatsApp text on our departmental account
	⁃	Ticket is captured and recorded by our help desk technician 
	⁃	The technician will then alert technicians of new ticket - focus will be on “Unassigned” technicians 
	⁃	Assign the ticket to technicians
